WEB WIZARDS ARENA
  1. 25th Oct 2023

Web Wizard Arena (WWA) was an exciting web development competition for students, featuring two intense groups: Group A for first and second-year students, and Group B for third and fourth-year experts. With 27 teams and a max of three members per team, the arena was ablaze! In a thrilling 3-hour showdown, teams tackled spontaneous problem statements, showcasing their web development skills. The top 4 teams from each group impressed judges Prof Shailesh Galande and Prof Madhura Kalabhor with their design, functionality, innovation, and execution. Web Wizard Arena: where digital dreams faced fierce competition! 🚀🌐 #WebWizardArena #TechShowdown

QUIZ COMPETITION FOR NATIONAL SCIENCE DAY
  1. 28th Feb 2024

ACM-W PCCOE and Institute Innovation Council (IIC) collaborated to host a dynamic Quiz Competition at Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering, Pune, in honor of National Science Day. The event comprised two rounds: an Online Quiz Round followed by an Offline Quiz Fire Round. Participants demonstrated their knowledge and enthusiasm for Science and Technology. The top performers from each round competed in a live session, with the top three teams emerging as winners. This event celebrated the essence of inquiry and innovation, fostering scientific curiosity, teamwork, and camaraderie among the students of PCCOE, Pune.
